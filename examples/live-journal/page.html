journal of the run
Recorded journal

The journal of one run, played back frame by frame. A claim is accepted only with a quote: exact bytes of a document. When a text is withdrawn, everything that rested on it falls and stays here with the reason. What you see at a frame is what the kernel itself gives for that frame.

  • Stands: accepted, and every ground under it stands.
  • Fell: lost a ground. It stays on the page with the reason.
  • Withdrawn: a quote whose text was taken back.
  • No longer stands: its bytes went to a new version, its grounds no longer support it, or it never stood.
  • Seal: the roots of the journal up to this frame.

One cell is one part: a paragraph, a list item or a table row. A part counts once the kernel closes the reading task, unless that reading was given up with nothing recorded and no reread recorded anything either: then the part shows as reading given up, not read.
